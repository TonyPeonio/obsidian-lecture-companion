- **Always take the best match.** A new slide-matching mode attaches the top-ranked deck without asking, even when the ranking is close (*Automatic* still asks in that case). Pick it in *Settings → Canvas → Match slides to the lecture*, in the Slides dropdown of the import and split dialogs, or with the picker's new **Always take the best match** button. Useful when splitting an old note into many lectures and you just want each one to get its most likely deck.
