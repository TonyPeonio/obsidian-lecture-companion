- **Meetings.** Tick **Meeting** next to the name in the panel (or in any import dialog) to record a work, club or 1:1 meeting instead of a class. Meetings go to `Meetings/<name>/<date>.md` with a series note that lists them and keeps a TODO list, are never looked up in Canvas (no slide matching, no "no Canvas course matches" notice, no course alias saved), have no Slides section, and get a meeting summary: Overview, Discussion, Decisions, Action items and Open questions. Action items are also added to the series TODO list. Manage the list and folder under *Settings → Meetings*.
- **Stricter course matching.** A typed name no longer matches a Canvas course through a year, term or filler word ("Club meeting 2026" used to match "Fall 2026 …"). A loose match needs at least half the meaningful words and one clear winner, and partial names must match from the start of a word.
- **Summarize this lecture note** now takes the course and date from the open note instead of the last recording.
