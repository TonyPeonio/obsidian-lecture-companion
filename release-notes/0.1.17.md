Summaries that only say what was said, measured; flashcards; the Ollama test button fixed.

**Measured**
- New `npm run check-summaries`: scores every `[mm:ss]` citation in a summary against the transcript (a transcript line within 20 s that shares a content word). On the test vault's old summaries it found 2 of 30 citations supported: the model had summarised the slides and invented a timeline. With this release's prompt and slide cap the same lecture scores 12 of 12.

**Changed**
- Summary prompts: every bullet, definition, example and announcement ends with a `[mm:ss]` copied from the transcript, never rounded or invented; slide text may sharpen names and terms but is not summarised on its own; sentence case.
- Slide text handed to the model is capped at max(3000 characters, 3 × the transcript's length), keeping the slides that overlap the transcript. A transcript under 45 seconds or 300 characters is not summarised; a Notice says so instead of producing a summary of the slides.

**New**
- **Flashcards for Spaced Repetition** (setting, off by default): after the summary, the review questions are answered from the transcript and written as `Question::Answer` lines under `## Flashcards` with the `#flashcards` tag, replaced on re-run.

**Fixed**
- *Test* next to the Ollama URL failed with "405 method not allowed": `/api/version` and `/api/tags` are GET-only and were called with POST.
- A model without a thinking mode is retried without the `think` flag; a missing model says `ollama pull <model>`; an unreachable server mentions `ollama serve`.

**Docs**
- README names the Companion suite (Local Vault Agent, Canvas, Exam and Paper Companion) and the new command.
